The project is managed using the Agile Scrum methodology.
The work is organized into Sprints, Epics, and User Stories.

### 1 Sprint
Epic 00: Offer Consultation

US 00: As a user, I will be able to view job offers.

Epic 01: Search Handling

US 01: As an Apprenant, I will be able to search for offers.

US 02: As an Apprenant, I will be able to sort the offers.

US 03: As an Apprenant, I will be able to filter the offers.

Epic 02: Offer Interaction

US 04: As an Apprenant, I will be able to follow offers.

US 05: As an Apprenant, I will be able to unfollow offers.

US 06: As an Apprenant, I will be able to view offer details.

Epic 03: Offer Management

US 07: As an administrator, I will be able to create offers.

US 08: As an administrator, I will be able to update offers.

US 09: As an administrator, I will be able to delete offers.

US 10: As an administrator, I will be able to associate technologies with an offer.
